# Droneer Website Introduction

This document is compiled from public information on the Droneer official store (shop.droneer.com) and its official blog, introducing the brand background, product lineup, and services of the website.

## About the Website

Droneer is an FPV (First-Person View) drone brand launched by Zhimu Technology. The official store serves as the brand's e-commerce site, offering [FPV drone](https://shop.droneer.com/collections/fpv-drones) airframes and related accessories to pilots, content creators, and industry operators worldwide, covering a wide range of flying scenarios from entry-level to freestyle and long-range flying. The site describes itself as one of the world's leading FPV technology brands.

## Brand Positioning

- Focused on advanced FPV (first-person view) drone technology, with rapid growth in recent years;
- Products target three groups: hobbyist beginners, professional filmmakers, and industry operators;
- Direct sales of complete drones and parts, with an emphasis on fast delivery and worldwide shipping.

## Product Lineup

- **C2501 Cinewhoop (2.5-inch)**: A classic cinewhoop design with full-enclosure injection-molded propeller guards. Lightweight (approx. 189g) with a 1.6W VTX and zero-latency performance, ideal for indoor and cinematic close-range filming. From approx. $239.00 for the Analog version; the O4 Pro version starts at approx. $379.00 (supports 4K/120fps).
- **X501 (5-inch Freestyle)**: The flagship model of the 5-inch series, designed for carefree freestyle flying ("Flip Without Fear") with agile, responsive handling.
- **X701 ([Long-Range drone](https://shop.droneer.com/collections/long-range-fpv-drones))**: Built for long-range flight ("Born for Long Range"), upgraded with an H743 dual-barometer and gyro flight controller. The PNP version is approx. $379.00.

## Versions & Buying Guide

- **PNP version**: For pilots who prefer to pair their own video transmitter, radio, and other gear — highly customizable;
- **Analog version**: Analog video transmission, the best value-for-money option;
- **O4 Pro version**: Flagship configuration with digital HD video transmission for stronger performance and image quality;
- Flight controller setup: the C2501 uses the mature F722 chip, while the X501 and X701 are upgraded to the more powerful H743 MCU for better processing power and stability, allowing GPS and various sensors to be mounted;
- The site features a Buying Guides section with practical content such as 2.5"/5"/7" comparisons, BNF & PNP checklists, and version selection guides.

## Services & Guarantees

- Official store direct sales with global ordering and fast shipping;
- One-stop shopping for complete drones and spare parts;
- Comprehensive buying guides and product comparisons for both beginners and professional users.
